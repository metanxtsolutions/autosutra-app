Audit date: 24 September 2026. Method: read-only fetches of raw HTML with curl (desktop Chrome UA), parsed with a stdlib Python extractor; 32 URLs fetched, 29 fully analysed. No forms submitted, no cookies accepted, no files in this repository modified. Where something could not be determined from the public HTML it is marked as such.

## 3. SEO practices observed

**Done well**
- Clean, shallow, keyword-bearing URL taxonomy (`/loans/{product}`, `/loans/{product}/{city}`, `/lenders/{lender}`, `/glossary/{term}`, `/guides/{slug}`) with a hub page at every level; hubs carry `CollectionPage` + `ItemList`.
- Unique, hand-written titles (25–59 chars) and meta descriptions (134–157 chars) on all 29 pages; product titles carry the headline rate; the rates page carries the current month.
- Exactly one H1 per page; consistent H2 template on product pages that doubles as an anchor-linked "On this page" TOC.
- Self-canonical everywhere, tidy redirect hygiene (www, http, trailing slash, `/blog`), true 404 status with `noindex` and a useful recovery page, case-sensitive 404s rather than soft 200s.
- Static prerendering: all content and schema in the initial HTML, cached at the edge.
- Breadcrumbs (visible + schema) on every inner page, including 4-level breadcrumbs on product×city pages.
- Dense, purposeful internal linking beyond the chrome: product ↔ city ↔ lender ↔ guide ↔ glossary cross-links ("Where we place files", "Products mentioned in this guide", "Related guides", "Related terms", "Where it applies", "Other private banks on the panel", cross-sell "a different product is cheaper").
- Per-route generated OG images with full OG/Twitter tag sets; `article:*` meta on guides.
- Strong security headers and a manifest; Search Console verified.

**Weak**
- Homepage and most hub H1s are taglines with no query terms ("Better loans begin with the right partner.", "The panel behind every shortlist.", "Straight answers.", "Talk to a person."); the `<title>` does all the keyword work.
- 112 product×city pages that are ~2/3 identical to their siblings; the FAQ block on them is a city-level FAQ that is not about the product on the page.
- Every page emits ~95–170 internal links (~56 from chrome alone, plus 16 city links and 18–19 partner-programme links even on borrower pages), diluting link equity and topical focus.
- HTML weight 120–295 KB with the content serialized twice (HTML + RSC payload); no images means no image-search surface at all.
- Sitemap `lastmod` is a build timestamp, not a content date; `priority`/`changefreq` add nothing.
- `meta keywords` still emitted; 404 page carries two robots metas and a canonical to the homepage; single self-referencing hreflang without `x-default`.
- Schema `image` properties (Organization.image, Article.image) point to the un-hashed `/opengraph-image` path which returns 404, while the real OG tags use hashed URLs that resolve.
- Generated heading strings have pluralisation/casing bugs ("companys", "nbfcs", "cas").
- Rate numbers embedded in titles ("from 10.25%") will churn or go stale.

## 4. AEO practices observed

- **FAQ blocks everywhere**: home (6), every hub, product (5–6), product×city (3), city (3), guide (3), lender (3), partner pages (6–8), calculators (4), rates (4), plus a 67-question FAQ hub. Built with native `<details>/<summary>` (works without JS), the answer text is identical to the `FAQPage` JSON-LD where checked (home 6/6, guide 3/3, calculator 4/4). Answers are 1–4 sentences, open with a verdict word ("No.", "Yes.", "Usually yes, but…"), and include numbers.
- **Direct-answer openings**: guides start with a 2-sentence definition immediately under the H1/byline; glossary terms start with a one-sentence definition; product pages put a rate/amount/tenure fact strip above the fold and a one-line answer under each H2 before bullets.
- **Comparison tables**: homepage "bank branch vs aggregator portal vs LoansPartner" Yes/No matrix; guide tables (direct-with-lender vs platform; payout % by product); the rates page's 12-row product table with a rationale sentence per row; product pages' EMI grid (amount × tenure).
- **Steps**: numbered 01–04 process on home, 01–05 on product pages, 01–06 on partner pages (with `HowTo` schema on `/partner`), 4-level grievance escalation.
- **Question-style headings**: used mostly in FAQ summaries and guide titles ("Which Is Cheaper for You?", "When It Saves Money and When It Does Not"); body H2s are mostly short declaratives ("Who qualifies", "What to keep ready", "What changes in Mumbai") that still read as implicit questions.
- **Freshness cues**: month-stamped rates page (title, H1, "repo rate 5.25% as of September 2026"), visible Published/Updated on guides, "Last updated" on the disclaimer.
- **Definitions layer**: 40-term glossary with `DefinedTerm`/`DefinedTermSet`, related terms and links to the products where each term applies.
- **Gaps**: no question-form H2s on product pages; FAQ schema covers only 18 of 67 questions on the FAQ hub; product×city FAQs are not product-specific; no `speakable`; `HowTo` and broad `FAQPage` markup no longer earn Google rich results for a commercial site (Google restricted both in 2023), so their value is limited to machine readability.

## 5. GEO / entity practices observed

- **Single canonical Organization entity** on every page: `@type ["Organization","FinancialService"]`, `@id …/#organization`, `legalName "LoansPartner Financial Services"`, `foundingDate 2019`, `slogan`, `telephone`, `email`, full `PostalAddress` (Kolkata), `areaServed` (India + 16 `City` nodes), two `contactPoint`s with `hoursAvailable` and `availableLanguage ["en","hi"]`, `logo` (512×512 PNG, resolves), `sameAs` (Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn — existence of those profiles not verified in this audit).
- **Entity graph wiring**: `WebSite` (`publisher` → org @id), every `WebPage` has `isPartOf` → website @id and `about` → org @id; `Service.provider`, `LoanOrCredit.provider`, `Article.publisher`, `WebApplication.provider`, `FinancialService.parentOrganization` all reference the same @id.
- **Typed pages**: AboutPage, ContactPage, CollectionPage, FAQPage, Article, DefinedTerm, WebApplication, HowTo, Service + LoanOrCredit (with amount, interestRate, loanTerm ranges).
- **Authorship**: Article `author` is an `Organization` "LoansPartner Credit Desk" with a description and URL → `/about`; visible byline "Published … Updated … Reviewed by LoansPartner Credit Desk"; a reviewer box at the end of each guide; the About page has a "Who writes and reviews our guides" section. **No `Person` entities, no named staff, no author pages, no credentials, no LinkedIn profiles of individuals.**
- **About page depth**: 1,382 words; founding story, stats strip, six operating principles, regulatory position ("what we are, and are not"), lender list. No team, no photos, no registration numbers (CIN/GSTIN), no lender empanelment codes, no awards/press, no testimonials, no ratings, no `aggregateRating`.
- **Third-party entity pages**: 26 lender pages with editorial ("Where HDFC Bank fits") and one outbound link each; 16 city pages; 40 glossary terms. Lender pages do not include an `Organization` node for the lender.
- **Citations**: guides reference RBI directions, bureaus and the KFS by name but contain **zero outbound links** to primary sources; the grievance page names `cms.rbi.org.in` and `cybercrime.gov.in` as unlinked text.
- **Trust/compliance framing** (consistent across footer, About, Disclaimer, Grievance): "we are a DSA/LSP, we do not lend", "loans decided solely by the lender under RBI directions", "we never charge borrowers", KFS emphasis, DPDP Act, working-hours contact policy, fraud warning with the 1930 helpline, 4-level grievance process with SLAs and the RBI Integrated Ombudsman.
- **Dates**: `datePublished`/`dateModified` on Article, `dateModified` on most WebPage nodes (2026-09-01), visible dates on guides/rates/disclaimer, none on product/city/lender pages.
- **Machine-access files**: no `llms.txt`, no RSS, no `security.txt`.

## 6. Weaknesses and things NOT to copy

1. **City × service matrix built from templates.** 112 pages at ~0.67 similarity with a reused, off-topic FAQ (home-loan questions on a personal-loan page). This is the classic thin/doorway pattern; only replicate where each location page has genuinely local content and a real local presence.
2. **Fabricated local entities.** Each city page emits a `FinancialService "LoansPartner Mumbai"` node with the Kolkata HQ address and the same phone. Emitting LocalBusiness-style nodes for cities without an office is misleading and a risk under Google's structured-data policies.
3. **Anonymous expertise.** "LoansPartner Credit Desk" as an Organization author, "former bank and NBFC credit professionals" with no names, bios, or verifiable credentials. Weak for E-E-A-T and for AI systems trying to attribute expertise.
4. **No primary-source citations** in YMYL-adjacent guides that repeatedly invoke RBI rules.
5. **Broken schema image URLs** (`Organization.image`, `Article.image` → 404) — verify every URL you put in JSON-LD.
6. **FAQ schema inconsistency** (18 of 67 questions on the FAQ hub; generic FAQ reused across product×city pages) and heavy reliance on `FAQPage`/`HowTo` markup that no longer produces rich results for commercial sites.
7. **Tagline H1s** on home and hubs; keywords live only in `<title>`.
8. **Auto-generated copy bugs** ("companys", "nbfcs", "cas") — templated headings need a review pass.
9. **Link bloat**: ~56 chrome links plus 16 city and ~18 partner links on every page regardless of intent.
10. **Content serialized twice** (HTML + RSC) producing 200–300 KB pages, and no images at all (no lender logos, no people, no screenshots), which also removes any image-search or visual-trust surface.
11. **Build-time sitemap `lastmod`** and `meta keywords` — cosmetic, but signal a generated rather than maintained sitemap.
12. **Volatile numbers in titles** ("Rates from 10.25%") that require re-titling whenever rates move.
13. **No social proof of any kind** (testimonials, ratings, case studies, logos). For autosutra the lesson is the opposite of copying: use only real, attributable proof, never placeholders.

## 7. Transferable practices worth considering for autosutra.in

1. **Hub-and-spoke URL taxonomy with breadcrumbs + `BreadcrumbList` at every level** (`/services`, `/services/{service}`, `/resources/{slug}`, `/glossary/{term}`) — makes the site's topical structure legible to crawlers and LLMs alike.
2. **One Organization entity with a stable `@id`, referenced from every page** (`WebPage.about`, `Service.provider`, `Article.publisher`) with `legalName`, `foundingDate`, `address`, `contactPoint` hours, `sameAs` — the cheapest way to give AI systems a single, consistent entity to resolve.
3. **`Service` schema per service page with `provider` @id, `areaServed`, `serviceType`** (drop the price/offer) — matches how AI answer engines describe "who does X for Y".
4. **"On this page" anchor TOC on long service pages and guides** with a fixed H2 template (What you get / Who it suits / What we need from you / How it works / FAQs) — improves extractability and consistency across pages.
5. **Visible Published / Updated / Reviewed-by byline plus matching `Article` dates** — but with named `Person` authors, credentials and author pages, which is exactly what this site lacks.
6. **FAQ blocks built with `<details>/<summary>` whose text is mirrored 1:1 in `FAQPage` JSON-LD, 3–6 page-specific questions, each answer opening with a verdict** — machine-readable Q/A pairs even now that FAQ rich results are gone.
7. **A homepage comparison matrix** ("in-house marketing hire vs freelancer vs generalist agency vs autosutra") — a Yes/No table is the format most reliably lifted into AI answers.
8. **A dated benchmark page** ("Dealer marketing benchmarks, September 2026": CPL, cost per walk-in, lead-to-test-drive rates by channel) with a table, an "About these numbers" Q/A section and a monthly update note — a recurring freshness signal and a natural citation target.
9. **A glossary with `DefinedTerm`/`DefinedTermSet`, related terms, and "where it applies" links to services** (VDP, cost per walk-in, OEM co-op, DMS, lead attribution) — cheap long-tail coverage and entity definitions LLMs can quote.
10. **Persona landing pages** modelled on `/partner/for/{persona}` (e.g. `/for/multi-brand-dealers`, `/for/two-wheeler-dealers`, `/for/oem-regional-teams`) with a persona-specific FAQ and step list — high intent, low duplication if each is written fresh.
11. **"Related guides" + "Services mentioned in this guide" blocks at the end of every article** — closes the article ↔ service loop the same way this site does with products.
12. **Platform/partner entity pages** (the `/lenders/{lender}` pattern → `/platforms/meta-ads`, `/platforms/google-vehicle-ads`, `/platforms/cardekho`) with an editorial "where it fits" section, a FAQ, and an outbound link — only for platforms actually used.
13. **A "How we work / what we don't do / escalation" page** with SLAs (acknowledge in X days, resolve in Y, named escalation contact) — the grievance-page pattern translated into agency account-management terms, a strong trust signal for B2B buyers.
14. **Redirect and error hygiene**: www/apex and trailing-slash 308s, legacy path redirects, real 404 with `noindex` and service links.
15. **Sitewide footer regulatory/positioning paragraph** stating plainly what the company is and is not — for autosutra: independent agency, not an OEM or lead-marketplace, no fabricated results, how leads are attributed.
16. **Per-route generated OG images and complete OG/Twitter sets** — free social/AI-preview polish with Next.js `opengraph-image`.
17. **Skip** the city×service matrix, the per-city LocalBusiness nodes, the tagline H1s, and the anonymous "desk" author; **add** what this site lacks: named people, cited sources, real client proof, and an `llms.txt`.
